AML/KYC

SwapsCenter Is Always Committed to a Safe and Secure Platform

SwapsCenter prioritizes user safety and financial security. Our robust Anti-Money Laundering (AML) program is the foundation of this commitment. It ensures the integrity of our services and safeguards against illegal financial activities.



Here's how we achieve this:

Streamlined Registration

To use our exchange, users complete a secure and efficient registration process. This initial step requires essential information to verify your identity. This complies with AML regulations and reduces potential platform misuse.



Enhanced Verification

In specific situations, we may require additional verification, such as extra documentation or video calls. This typically occurs when the source of funds is unclear or a personal connection exists with a suspected sender.



Advanced Technology

Our system continuously monitors transactions for suspicious activity using sophisticated algorithms. This proactive approach helps identify transactions potentially linked to money laundering or other criminal acts.



Investigative Holds

If a transaction raises concerns, it may be temporarily placed on hold. This precaution allows SwapsCenter to thoroughly investigate the source of funds.

Transparency and Collaboration


Your Participation 

By agreeing to our Terms and using our services, you acknowledge and agree to cooperate fully with our security measures. This includes providing complete and accurate details about your funds' origin, especially during transaction holds or information requests.



Honesty is Key

Supplying truthful, current, and complete information is vital. You are solely responsible for the accuracy of the details provided. Remember, honesty and transparency are fundamental to a secure financial system.



Information Requests and Fund Freezing

Failing to provide information or supplying false details can have serious repercussions. If information suggests potential criminal activity, SwapsCenter may freeze the funds temporarily.
